## The shape of it

One sponsored placement at a time, always marked Sponsored, kept
separate from the directory's listings. Sponsorship never buys a
listing, a review or a category position: those stay editorial and free.

Money for the directory is planned to run through [Open
Collective](https://opencollective.com/) with a public ledger, and a
fixed share of it (target: 20%) is meant to flow back to the listed
projects themselves. What is live versus planned is spelled out on the
[funding page](/funding/).

## Status: not open yet

Sponsorship is not open today; the audience comes first, and this page
gets updated when placements open. If you might want one when they do,
or want to talk it through sooner, reach us through the [FOSSadmin
contact page](https://fossadmin.com/contact/) (scan or click the code to
email us) and mention the index.
